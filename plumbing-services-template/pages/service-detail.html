Pipe Repair & Replacement

Comprehensive pipe diagnostics, copper joint soldering, PEX upgrades, and main line leak repairs backed by a 1-year labor warranty.

Professional Pipe Repair Services in Metro Area

Leaking, corroded, or burst pipes can cause catastrophic water damage to your flooring, drywall, and electrical systems in a matter of hours. At FlowFix Plumbing, our certified master plumbers utilize non-invasive acoustic sensors and thermal imaging to isolate hidden pipe fractures quickly without tearing up unnecessary walls.

Whether you require a single pinhole leak repair in a copper joint or a full home repiping project using durable flexible PEX tubing, we deliver clean, compliant workmanship designed to last for decades.

Warning Signs You Need Pipe Repair Immediately

Sudden unexplained drop in water pressure
Damp spots, mildew, or warm areas on floors
Rusty, brownish, or metallic tasting tap water
Spike in monthly municipal water utility bills
Continuous sound of running water when taps are off
Visible corrosion, green patina, or flaking on exposed pipes

Our 4-Step Pipe Repair Process

1

Acoustic & Thermal Inspection

We pinpoint the exact leak location without damaging drywalls using acoustic frequency locators.

2

Water Line Isolation & Upfront Quote

We isolate the damaged section, stop water flow, and present a guaranteed flat-rate price quote.

3

Precision Soldering or PEX Fitting

We replace damaged pipe sections with commercial-grade copper or flexible PEX piping according to building codes.

4

Pressure Testing & Clean-up

System is pressurized to verify 100% seal integrity. Work area is thoroughly sanitized and cleaned.
